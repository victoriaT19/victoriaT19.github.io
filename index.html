tokenprof: token profiler for LLM agents

Open source · research · 2026

A proxy that sits between an agent and any OpenAI-compatible API and logs every call: tokens, cache, cost and latency. Detectors then point at the waste, such as context resent on every call, broken prompt caches and loops.

  • Study of Gemma 4 coding agents (E2B and E4B) on the 129 public tasks of the Gemma 4 Developer Agent competition: 8 full runs, 1,032 attempts, one identical re-run as a noise control
  • About 40% of prompt tokens were a fixed prefix resent on every call; profiler-guided changes cut E2B's context overflows from 29% to 0% (Fisher's exact test, p < 0.01)
  • Written up as a technical paper with every trace published
  • Python
  • LLM agents
  • vLLM
  • LangGraph
  • Kaggle GPUs

Pit wall: F1 race strategy

Dashboard · LLM agent · 2026

An F1 strategy engineer built from public timing data and Pirelli's tyre nominations, updated after every session of the remaining 2026 races. It started as my 2025 Abu Dhabi finishing-order predictor.

  • Tyre degradation model, Monte Carlo pit-strategy comparison and a lap-by-lap simulation of the whole field (traffic, overtaking, safety cars, the undercut) with an animated on-track replay
  • Finishing order from a Plackett-Luce ranking model (driver and team strengths) ensembled with gradient boosting: Spearman 0.705 vs. 0.677 for the starting grid over 68 races, with win and podium chances scored by Brier
  • A race-engineer chat: Gemma 4 31B with tool calling over the same calculations, on a chain of free models that shows each tool call live
  • Leakage-free backtests, reported even when negative: pit timing is no better than a historical baseline, since a quarter of real stops come under safety cars

Resonance: Physics AI

Gemma 4 Good Hackathon · 2026

A physics tutor made by fine-tuning Google's Gemma 4 E2B to answer in two levels: the intuition first, then the derivation.

  • Distillation: 330 question–answer pairs written by Gemma 4 31B, filtered by automatic checks and a physics review, then QLoRA fine-tuning on a single T4 GPU
  • Base vs. tuned on 20 held-out questions with an LLM judge and a sign test: aggressive training made the physics worse (2.3 vs. 3.3, p = 0.049) despite a lower loss; the conservative version kept the physics and got the format right in every answer
  • Adapter and an interactive demo published on Hugging Face
